# The One-Line Laugh

Entry for the make.fun bounty "The One-Line Laugh".

The program writes ONE funny line — a single sentence of at most 200
characters — to `entry.txt`. The house judge reads the lines two at a
time and keeps the bigger laugh, weighing a real surprise at the end
that still makes sense, an everyday truth people recognise, and
economy of words.

Our line: **"My passwords are so secure even I can't log in, which makes me the only hacker I've ever beaten."** (96 characters)
